# Brooker Systems SEO + Content Agent Plan

## Purpose

Make Brooker Systems easier for humans, Google, Bing and AI answer systems to understand as a real Australian business that builds apps, agents and automations.

## Priority order

1. Service pages: pages for the things Brooker Systems sells.
2. Case studies: proof that the work is real.
3. Blog posts: articles that answer buyer questions and link back to service pages.
4. External signals: LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, testimonials, client mentions.

## Core service pages

- `/apps`: web apps, portals, product builds, dashboards and internal tools.
- `/agents`: AI-assisted workflows, supervised agents and operations agents.
- `/automations`: repeated computer work, APIs, reports and business processes.

Ad or campaign-specific pages may still exist, but the public site navigation should centre on the three service buckets above.

## Weekly content agent workflow

Run weekly. Do not publish automatically.

1. Pick one buyer-intent topic.
2. Research current examples or phrasing if needed.
3. Draft one article in Max's direct, practical voice.
4. Include:
   - title;
   - suggested slug;
   - meta description;
   - short answer at the top;
   - Australian or small-business context;
   - practical examples;
   - FAQ section;
   - internal links to relevant service pages;
   - LinkedIn post version.
5. Save draft under `docs/seo/drafts/`.
6. Send Max a short approval summary.
7. Publish only after explicit approval.

## First article topics

1. How much does a practical business app cost in Australia?
2. What can an AI agent actually do inside a small business?
3. What is an API integration and when does a business need one?
4. When should a business replace spreadsheets with an internal tool?
5. Custom app vs no-code vs spreadsheet: what should a small business use?
6. How construction businesses can automate quoting, reporting and repeated admin.
7. How to validate an app idea before building the full product.
8. What to prepare before asking someone to build your internal software.

## Monthly audit checklist

- Check every important page has a unique title and meta description.
- Check schema exists on homepage, service pages and articles.
- Check sitemap includes new pages.
- Check robots.txt references sitemap.
- Check internal links from homepage to service pages.
- Check service pages link to relevant articles and case studies.
- Check broken links.
- Check Google Business Profile and LinkedIn consistency once those exist.
- Identify new case-study candidates from recent work.

## Content rules

- Avoid generic AI SEO filler.
- Never use em dashes.
- Write around buying questions and real business problems.
- Prefer concrete examples over vague claims.
- Keep pricing language clear where useful.
- Link every article to at least one service page.
- Do not claim deep platform-specific expertise unless true. Phrase it as API and systems automation capability.
